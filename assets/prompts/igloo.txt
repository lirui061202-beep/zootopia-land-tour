A row of four small attached shopfronts, one to two storeys, playful and varied.
1 (leftmost): a tattoo studio whose entrance is a big horseshoe-shaped red-brick arch covered with black line-drawn doodle ornaments, a polished brass door inside the arch, small number plate.
2: a hairdresser for sheep: smooth white stucco with a periwinkle-blue base, a purple wavy cornice, a round-topped shop window with tree-branch mullions, door surround made of colourful button mosaic, a red-white-blue barber pole.
3: a wool and knitwear shop in soft cream with lilac trim, shop window full of yarn balls and sweaters, a rounded knitted-texture awning in pink and teal stripes.
4 (rightmost): a taller nail salon in pink with a round pink blank sign shaped like a paw pad cluster replaced by a plain pink disc, green leafy textured upper wall with a green artichoke-shaped dome canopy and small yellow windows with purple trim.
