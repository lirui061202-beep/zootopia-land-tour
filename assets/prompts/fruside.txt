ONE long two-storey building: the side elevation of an elegant pastel pink boutique. Salmon-pink stucco with cream rococo scroll trim, a wavy cream parapet with two small curled gables, round porthole windows with dark red frames on the upper floor, three large arched display windows on the ground floor with gold frames showing tiny dresses and hat boxes on stands, a double exit door in cream and gold at the right end under a small pink scalloped canopy, cascades of purple wisteria hanging from the cornice at the left end, a white rounded base course with two tiny blue mouse-sized doors, gold wall lamps between the windows.
