Three buildings in a row.
LEFT: a two-storey shop with deep purple and lavender vertical pilasters, a gold-and-brown rounded awning roof over a glass shopfront full of colourful goods, blue patterned tile base; above it a recessed balcony with a rounded pale blue building behind.
MIDDLE (the hero): a very tall, very narrow tower building for giraffes, taller than everything else, leaning slightly, its whole facade painted like giraffe skin: large rounded orange-brown patches on warm yellow separated by thick cream outlines; two small round porthole windows near the top with cream rings, one round wooden vent, an extremely tall slender wooden door (four storeys high, narrow) with a long vertical handle, cream stone frame, and a dark iron horseshoe-shaped ornament standing on the flat top of the tower.
RIGHT: a three-storey shop with olive green and lime curved overhanging roofs (like stacked leaves), pink and red window frames, blank cream billboard panel on the roof, a round window, purple flower boxes, a white round badge-shaped blank sign near the ground.
