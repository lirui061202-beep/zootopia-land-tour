ONE single symmetrical monumental building, centred in the image with empty magenta on both sides: the city hall of the animal metropolis. Warm golden sandstone. Its silhouette is a tall organic pointed form like a sail or a termite-mound spire: a slender central tower rising to a needle spire, and from high on the tower two long concave swooping walls curve outward and down to the left and right like a wide skirt, their top edges trimmed with a thick coral-red scalloped band. Running down the middle of the central tower is a long teardrop-shaped recess holding a rounded glass column with horizontal bands of deep blue and white glass. Below, where the skirt flattens, a broad flat curved sandstone canopy projects over the entrance, with a long teal-blue glazed mosaic band under it (the band is blank, no lettering). Under the canopy: a recessed entrance with tall glass doors between smooth rounded sandstone piers, wide shallow steps. The building is about 1.3 times taller than it is wide.
