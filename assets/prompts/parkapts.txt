A row of five attached apartment houses, each a different animal-sized home.
1 (leftmost): ochre-yellow stucco, three storeys, on a rough sandstone rock base, with one huge arched plank door framed by alternating blue and cream voussoir stones, sky-blue louvred shutters on big square windows, rows of round log-ends studding the wall, potted agaves on a ledge, a green water tank on the flat roof, and a tiny mouse door at the base.
2: a very narrow taller tower of banded pink and cream stone with a vertical column of many tiny blue arched windows for rodents.
3: a cream and periwinkle-blue polygonal bay turret with two arched openings side by side at the top, and at the base three nested front doors of three sizes (wood with blue Art Nouveau glass) under white globe lamps.
4: red-brown brick with one big oval window ringed in brick with a green frame, and small green sash windows with grey stone lintels.
5 (rightmost): moss-green rough leafy stucco with stacked round balconies - the lowest shaped like a lotus-leaf bowl, the next screened with bamboo poles - and wavy teal trim, a clothesline with colourful laundry.
