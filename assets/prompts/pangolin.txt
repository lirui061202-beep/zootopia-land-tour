A row of three attached buildings on a side street.
LEFT: a slightly shabby five-storey brick apartment house in muted olive and brown with a fire-escape of green iron, small mismatched windows, a rounded scaly roof parapet like pangolin armour plates, and an arched entrance with a blank brass plate.
MIDDLE: a climbing gym with a grey concrete facade shaped with large sculpted curved claw forms gripping the wall, painted palm-frond mural, big glass window.
RIGHT: an Art Deco hotel in cream and dusty rose for hibernating animals, with rows of small rounded capsule windows with blue shutters, a curved corner, a half-round entrance canopy with a crescent-moon ornament.
