ONE wide building: the city's natural history museum. Long two-and-a-half-storey facade of red-brown brick with warm sandstone trim. A central grand arched portal with bronze doors flanked by pairs of sandstone columns carved like stacked mammoth tusks, a shallow green copper dome above the portal. Along the brick wall on both sides: a row of cream bottle-shaped poster frames (posters are abstract colourful nature illustrations without any text or animals), round amber glass wall lamps between them, a sandstone base course, and a carved frieze of fossil ammonites and leaves under the cornice. Flat parapet with small stone finials.
