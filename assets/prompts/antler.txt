Three buildings in a row.
LEFT: a tall narrow mint-and-emerald-green Art Nouveau shop, three storeys, with a curved two-storey glass bay window on the corner, green rounded hood canopy over it, a big round porthole window in a thick green ring at the top under a small green dome with a pointed leaf finial, blank coloured poster panels on its side, wooden shop door.
MIDDLE: a four-storey warm sandstone-block tower with tall narrow arched windows with teal glass, a projecting cream corbelled oriel turret on the upper corner carrying a small round clock face, topped by a dark bronze dome crowned with a large sculpted pair of moose antlers; cream stone balustrade porch and steps at its base with a small arched cellar door.
RIGHT: a three-storey red-brick building with pink-and-cream striped arched window surrounds, big semicircular fan windows with green frames, an ornate bright green zig-zag patterned cornice across the top, an iron balcony with overflowing purple flowers, a rounded stone canopy over the entrance door and a green garage-style door, small palm trees growing on its flat roof.
