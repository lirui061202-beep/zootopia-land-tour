ONE single building filling the full width of the image: an elegant fashion boutique for a tiny arctic-shrew socialite, two and a half storeys tall. Salmon-pink stucco walls with thick cream-white rococo scroll trim. At the top a curvy baroque gable; set into the gable is a rounded dome-shaped panel upholstered in deep red diamond-quilted (button-tufted) pattern with a gold rim, crowned by a slender gold finial post. Below it round porthole windows with crimson frames. A white balustraded curved balcony with red flowers across the first floor, with tall arched crimson-framed windows behind it. The ground floor is a smooth white organic cave-like arched portal, thick and softly sculpted like piped cream, containing a wood-and-glass double door with Art Nouveau curves; beside the door a round blank gold medallion plaque and a small gold number plate; cascades of purple wisteria drape down the upper left wall; two tiny blue rodent-scale doors in the white base course.
