Three buildings in a row.
LEFT: a flower shop: ground floor is a green-painted timber shopfront with a terracotta barrel-tile pent roof, shop windows with playful rounded organic outlines, buckets of cut flowers on stepped shelves, a hanging carved wooden blank sign; above it two storeys of red-brown brick with clipped ivy and blue shuttered windows with flower boxes.
MIDDLE: an import bazaar: warm yellow stucco, three storeys, with one big Moorish horseshoe arch entrance on the ground floor whose edge is trimmed with a band of teal triangle tiles, carved floral plaster relief around the arch, hanging brass lanterns and rolled rugs visible inside, small arched windows above, a wavy stepped parapet striped orange and cream, small number plate.
RIGHT: an art supply store: old red brick wall with one very large arched window with a thin green frame showing easels and jars of brushes, a paint-splattered blank wooden signboard above, a bright blue door, a striped fabric awning in yellow and white.
