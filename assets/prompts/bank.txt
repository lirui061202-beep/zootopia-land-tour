Two attached buildings.
LEFT (narrow, about 35 percent of the width): a comedy club with a dark teal facade, a marquee canopy shaped like a big tropical leaf edged with round light bulbs (blank marquee panel), a ticket booth window and a red door.
RIGHT (wide, about 65 percent): a stately small bank built for lemmings and for big animals at once: three storeys of pale stone with fat pink-veined marble columns on tall plinths, a carved pediment, a green-framed arched window above, polished brass details; at ground level one big brass door and beside it a row of tiny brass revolving doors only ankle high with tiny brass plaques; gold cornice.
