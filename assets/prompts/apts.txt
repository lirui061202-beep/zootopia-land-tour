One large apartment complex made of three attached parts.
LEFT part: a yellow-ochre and olive-green Art Nouveau house, four storeys, with curved iron balconies, a rounded bay, green striped awning and a round window.
CENTRE part (the hero, widest): the upper storeys are stacked pastel cube modules like giant habitat boxes in lavender purple, teal, mint green and pink, arranged in a staggered grid three rows high, each cube with one big round porthole window set in thick concentric rounded rings (like front-loading washing-machine doors), a few cubes with small clear tubes connecting them, little shrubs and red-brown thatched tufts on the roof; the lower two storeys are red-brown brick with large horizontal oval windows in thick cream stone rings and a big round hobbit-style door in a cream swirl frame.
RIGHT part: a frosty pale-blue and white rounded building that looks carved from ice and snow, with smooth icy bulges, a round blue door, round frosted windows and a pale turquoise balustrade stair at its base.
