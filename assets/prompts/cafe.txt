ONE single building filling the full width of the image: an ice-cream parlour of elephant proportions - only ONE very tall storey. Peach and cream stucco with vertical cream-and-peach candy stripes on the walls. Red-pink pilasters divide the facade, each with a round gold medallion at the top; the base of every pilaster is sculpted like an elephant's foot with rounded toenails. A parapet with a scalloped arch relief along the top. Deep crimson fabric awnings with a cream wavy 'dripping ice-cream' edge hang on red metal brackets over each opening. In the centre an elephant-size crimson double door, and cut into the lower part of that door a small take-out window with a little counter. Large shop windows on either side showing giant sundae glasses and jars of ice lollies. Wall sconces shaped like ice lollies (popsicles). On the roof a blank red billboard on two posts.
