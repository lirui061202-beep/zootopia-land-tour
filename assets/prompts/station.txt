ONE single wide building centred in the image: the city's central railway station entrance. Sage-green and cream painted stucco with verdigris ironwork. A large semicircular arched gable spans almost the whole width, edged with scalloped leaf-shaped moulding, containing a row of seven tall round-headed stained-glass windows (tallest in the middle) glazed in multicoloured organic shapes of green, teal, orange and blue. Small rounded corner turrets on each side of the gable with tiny green domes. Under the windows a long flower-box ledge full of red and yellow flowers (a blank cream band below it, no lettering). At street level a deep green-and-cream striped scalloped fabric canopy across the full width, with three arched doorways of different sizes beneath it (one very tall, one medium, one tiny) and green iron lamp brackets.
