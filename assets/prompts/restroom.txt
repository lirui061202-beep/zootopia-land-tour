ONE long low building, one and a half storeys: public restrooms themed as a rainforest district. Rough lilac-purple stucco walls, mossy old brick at the base, big orange-brown tree-root shaped timbers curling over the wall and framing the openings, bamboo-pole eaves with a thatched leaf roof edge, round woven rattan lamps hanging under the eaves, large tropical leaves and ferns growing from pockets in the wall. Two entrances, each a set of nested doorways of different sizes one inside the other (an elephant-sized arch containing a human-sized door, beside it a tiny mouse door at the base and a small raised door high on the wall), with blank round wooden plaques above them.
